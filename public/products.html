Products

One product, and it is free

PixelFlow is a single web app. There are no tiers, no paid plan and no feature held back — everything below is included for anyone who signs up.

PixelFlow — study & focus. A pixel-art pomodoro timer with a weekly study plan, logged hours, and the analytics to tell whether the two matched.

Open PixelFlow

What is in it

Feature What it does Needed?
Pomodoro timer Focus sessions with break cycles. Name a topic and finished sessions add real minutes. Built in
Weekly plan Planned hours per weekday, editable directly, with preset chips and decimal hours (5.5 becomes 5h 30m). Built in
Study logs Manual entry or automatic from the timer, with tags. Edit any entry in place. Built in
Analytics Calendar heatmap, daily and weekly charts, streaks, XP and levels. Built in
Tasks & calendar To-dos with tags, plus events and deadlines. These never change a study total. Built in
Pixel avatar A companion that reacts to your pomodoro cycles, with a pose picker. Built in
Friends See who is online, what they are studying, and their week. Only for people you add. Optional
Google sign-in Sign in with an existing Google account instead of creating another. Optional
Google Calendar sync Read your events into the app, and write events you create in PixelFlow back to Google. Access is to events only. Optional
Spotify player Play your own playlists and tracks inside the app. Premium

What is not here

  • No paid tier. Nothing above is locked behind payment.
  • No advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No mobile app yet. PixelFlow runs in any browser, and your account follows you across devices once you sign in.
  • No bulk export yet. Deletion is instant; export is on the way.

Hosting and data

The app is served by Vercel and stores its database with Turso. Google and Spotify are contacted only if you connect them, and disconnecting removes the stored access immediately. Nothing else is sent anywhere.

The Privacy Policy has the detail, including every category of data held and how long it is kept.
