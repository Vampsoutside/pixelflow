Privacy Policy

What PixelFlow stores, why, who else sees it, and how to make it go away.

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Applies to stitch-flax.vercel.app

The short version. PixelFlow is a personal study tracker. Your study data belongs to you, we do not sell it, we do not show anyone ads, and we do not use analytics or third-party tracking. Connecting Google or Spotify is optional and you can disconnect at any time. Deleting your account deletes your data.

1. What this covers

This policy describes the PixelFlow web app. It does not cover Google or Spotify's own services — once you grant access, their privacy policies govern the data on their side.

2. What we collect

Everything below is stored on our server so the app can work. Nothing is collected in the background, and there is no third-party analytics script.

Social

If you connect with other users, we store that you are connected and, while the app is open, whether you are online, away or offline and what you are studying. Connected users can see your username, avatar, experience level, and your study minutes for today and this week — including your planned minutes. We do not show anyone your task lists, your calendar, your tags or your email address.

3. Optional connections

Both are optional. Nothing in PixelFlow requires them, and the app is fully usable without either.

Google (sign-in)

If you sign in with Google we store Google's immutable user id and the email address on your Google account, so we can recognise you next time. We do not read your Gmail, Drive or contacts.

Google Calendar (sync)

If you connect your calendar, we store an access token and a refresh token, plus the title, date and time of the events we sync. We request access to events only. We cannot see your calendar list, its sharing settings, or any calendar other than the one you connect. You can disconnect at any time, which deletes the stored tokens immediately.

Spotify (playback)

If you connect Spotify we store an access token and a refresh token. Playback happens inside Spotify's own player, which is subject to Spotify's privacy policy.

4. Who else can see your data

Only the infrastructure providers needed to run the app, all of whom process data solely on our instructions:

  • Vercel — hosts the app and serves its files.
  • Turso — hosts the database where your records are stored.
  • Google — sign-in, and calendar sync if you connect it.
  • Spotify — playback, if you connect it.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it for anyone else's advertising. We do not use advertising or analytics networks.

5. Cookies

PixelFlow sets three cookies, all strictly necessary and none of which track you across sites:

CookiePurpose
pf_sessionKeeps you signed in. HttpOnly, so scripts cannot read it.
pf_session_csrfProtects against cross-site request forgery.
pf_consentRecords that you answered the cookie banner, so we do not ask again.

On your first visit we ask whether you accept these. Declining does not stop the app working — the session cookies are set by the server and are required to sign you in at all. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings, which signs you out.

6. How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed immediately — see below.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes:

  • your account, username, email and avatar;
  • all study logs, plans, pomodoro sessions, tasks, tags and calendar items;
  • your friendships and presence;
  • your stored Google and Spotify tokens and any linked provider identity.

Deletion is immediate and your access ends at the same time — a stale session cannot keep working afterwards. This is not a request queue; there is nothing to wait for.

To disconnect only a provider, without deleting your account, use the disconnect button beside it in Settings.

What we cannot delete for you

Events already synced into Google Calendar from PixelFlow, and your Spotify account, live in those services. Disconnecting stops all further access; to remove an event entirely, revoke PixelFlow's access from your Google account permissions page or from your Spotify account settings.

8. Security

  • Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes, never in plain text.
  • Sessions are signed cookies that are HttpOnly and SameSite-restricted.
  • Provider tokens are stored server-side and never sent to the browser.
  • State-changing requests require a CSRF token, so another site cannot act as you.
  • The production server refuses to start without a signing secret configured.

No system is perfect. If you believe your account has been compromised, delete the account and create a new one.

9. Children

PixelFlow is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or why, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.

11. Contact

Questions, or a deletion request?

Use the contact address in the app's Settings page. Because deletion is self-service, that same place is the fastest way to have anything removed.
