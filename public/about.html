PixelFlow

A pixel-art pomodoro timer that actually keeps track of your study time.

PixelFlow helps you plan how long you intend to study, time what you really study, and see whether the two matched. Your progress follows you across devices, and you can connect a Google Calendar so your schedule sits next to your study hours.

This page describes the app for visitors and reviewers. You do not need an account to read it.

What PixelFlow does

Pomodoro timer

A focus timer with break cycles. You name what you are working on, and each finished session adds real minutes to your log.

Study hours and weekly plan

Set planned hours for each day of the week, then log what you actually studied. The app shows totals, streaks, and whether you hit each day's target.

Heatmaps and charts

A calendar heatmap and trend charts make it obvious where the gaps are, without turning your data into a spreadsheet.

Tasks, tags and calendar

Keep to-dos, tag them, and put events and deadlines on a calendar. These items never change a study total — they sit alongside your hours.

Google Calendar sync

Optional. Connect your Google Calendar to see your events on the right days and keep the same schedule on every device. Disconnect whenever you like.

Study with friends

Add friends to see who is online, what they are studying, and how everyone's week is going. Only people you have connected can see this.

Who it is for

PixelFlow is for students, self-learners and anyone working through something over weeks rather than minutes — exam revision, a language, a course, a thesis, or a daily practice. If you want to know whether the time you spent matched the time you meant to spend, that is the whole idea.

It is a self-tracking tool. It does not provide courses, tutoring or academic advice — your logged hours are your own record.

How your data is handled

Your study data belongs to you. We do not sell it, we do not show advertising, and we do not use analytics or third-party tracking.

Connections are optional. Google sign-in, Google Calendar sync and Spotify playback are all optional, and the app works fully without them.

You can delete everything. Settings has a Delete account button that removes your account and all of its data immediately.

The Privacy Policy sets out exactly what is stored and why.

Using PixelFlow

You can sign up with a username and password, continue as a guest and claim the account later, or sign in with Google. Your calendar and music connections are yours to add or remove at any time, and disconnecting one leaves the rest of your account alone.
