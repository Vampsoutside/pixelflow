What PixelFlow does

A study tool for anyone working through something over weeks rather than minutes — exam revision, a language, a course, a thesis, or a daily practice.

⏱

Pomodoro timer

Focus sessions with break cycles. Name what you are working on and every finished session adds real minutes to your log.

📊

Study hours and weekly plan

Set planned hours for each day, then log what you actually studied. Totals, streaks and whether you hit each day's target.

🔥

Heatmaps and charts

A calendar heatmap and trend charts make the gaps obvious, without turning your study life into a spreadsheet.

✅

Tasks, tags and calendar

Keep to-dos, tag them, and put events and deadlines on a calendar. These never move a study total — they sit alongside your hours.

📅

Google Calendar sync

Optional. Connect your Google Calendar to see your events on the right days and keep one schedule across devices. Disconnect whenever you like.

👥

Study with friends

Add friends to see who is online, what they are studying, and how everyone's week is going. Only people you have connected can see it.

Your data belongs to you

No advertising. No analytics. No selling your data.

Connections are optional. Google sign-in, Google Calendar sync and Spotify playback are all optional — the app is fully usable without any of them.

Delete everything, any time. Settings has a Delete account button that removes your account and all of its data immediately, not on request.

Read the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored and why, or the Terms of Use for the rules.
