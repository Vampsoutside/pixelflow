Support

Help, and how to reach us

Most problems are answered below. If yours is not, the fastest route is the support address — we answer support email directly.

Common questions

I forgot my password

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, so we cannot read yours or email it back. If you signed up with Google, sign in with Google instead. Otherwise you can continue as a guest and claim a new account — your old data stays behind unless you delete it.

My study data looks wrong

Two things move totals in PixelFlow: logging study minutes (Settings or the Logs tab) and finishing a pomodoro session. Events, deadlines and tasks never change a study total. If you logged the same hours twice, remove one of the entries from the Logs tab and the totals correct themselves.

Can I plan hours per specific week?

Planned hours are a weekly template — the same plan applies to every week, which is why changing your view to another date does not change your plan. Editing a specific week only is not supported yet.

Google Calendar sync is not working

Work through these in order:

  • Open the Calendar tab. If it says NOT CONNECTED, the Google connection lapsed — press Connect Google Calendar and grant access again.
  • Press Sync. It reports how many events were added or updated. If it says it could not reach Google, the connection is fine and Google is the problem — try again in a minute.
  • Events deleted in Google are not removed from PixelFlow automatically.

Does the app need my Google password?

No. Sign-in uses Google's own consent screen. PixelFlow never sees your Google password, and the Calendar connection only grants access to events — not your sharing settings, not your list of calendars, and no other account.

Can I export or move my data?

Not as a bulk download yet. Your records are yours, and you can delete everything at any time from Settings → Delete account. If you need a full export, ask us and we will arrange one.

The site is slow or a page is broken

Try a hard refresh first. If it persists, tell us your browser and roughly when it started — that is usually enough to reproduce it.

Account and privacy questions

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete account. Immediate, and it also releases your Google and Spotify connections.
  • Disconnect one service only: Settings → Disconnect, next to that service. Your PixelFlow account is untouched.
  • What is stored: the Privacy Policy, which lists every category and why.

Contact us

Email: support@pixelflow.app

For account deletion specifically, the fastest route is self-service — Settings → Delete account — because that removes your data immediately rather than waiting on a reply.

Please do not send passwords, tokens or API keys by email. We do not need them and would not use them.

Reporting a security issue

If you have found something that lets someone access data they should not, email security@pixelflow.app with what you found and how to reproduce it. We would rather hear about it than not.
